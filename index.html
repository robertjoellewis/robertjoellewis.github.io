Welcome to StoryCabin Canyon Retreat

Our white, light-filled cabin on Canyon Lake’s quiet north side was designed for unhurried mornings, easy evenings, and a relaxed flow the moment you arrive. Slip off your shoes, pour a cup, and let Hill Country pace take over.

About the Artwork

The images in the downstairs bathroom are pictographs from Paint Rock, Texas, just north of the Concho River. It's about a 3-hour drive from here, and the landowners offer regular tours through their Facebook page if you're up for an early morning adventure. I've been told it's the largest collection of pictographs in North America.

The artwork on the TV wall downstairs is from a few different artists, all based in Austin. I'm trying to collect more local work from this area and San Antonio, so tips are appreciated!

House Guidelines

Recycling & Trash

  • Place recyclables in the large outdoor can or the blue pantry container—choose what’s easiest.
  • If a trash bag is full, please place it in the plastic crate outside the glass door and close the lid. If it's less than half full, leave it inside.

Home Rhythm

  • The thermostat at the top of the stairs is yours to adjust; please set it back to 72° when you depart.
  • Stream freely and sign out of personal apps before checkout.
  • Use the Turkish blanket for pets and keep the white bedding paw-free.

Explore the Neighborhood

Hill Country Triangle

Blanco, Gruene, and Wimberley are each a 20-minute scenic drive—ideal for antiques, live music, and relaxed dining.

Favorite swims: Blanco River at Blanco State Park, Landa Springs in New Braunfels, and Cypress Creek Falls in Wimberley.

Local Bite

WildFlour is minutes away—seasonal plates, not too pricey, relaxed ambiance.

Groceries & Essentials

Brookshire Brothers sits right around the corner—ideal for basics, last-minute staples, and prepared bites (selection is tighter and pricing a touch higher than H-E-B).

For a fuller cart, the closest H-E-B is in Wimberley. New Braunfels (4 minutes longer) has a much more diverse selection.

Neighborhood Trail

A one-mile neighborhood stroll lands you at a peaceful trailhead: Open the map.

Water & Sky

When Overlook Park is open, stroll across the Canyon Lake Dam at sunset—romantic and breezy. Gates sometimes close; a quick “Overlook Park Canyon Lake” search gives the latest.

For a cool-down, head to the Guadalupe River trail below the dam and dip your toes in the release water.

Safety & Essentials

  • Emergency? Dial 911. The closest emergency room is in New Braunfels.
  • Verify burn-ban status before striking the fire ring: Comal County Fire Marshal.
  • First-aid kit is in the downstairs bathroom cabinet; kitchen upper cabinets hold everyday remedies.
  • If you move any outdoor pieces, place them back before checkout to keep pathways clear.

Checkout Checklist

  • Thermostat back to 72°.
  • Backyard string lights off, remote returned to the Crown Royal bag.
  • If a trash bag is full, place it in the plastic crate outside the glass door and close the lid. If it's less than half full, leave it inside.
  • Log out of any streaming apps you used.
  • Give the kitchen and living areas a quick tidy so the next story can start fresh.
  • If you ran the washer/dryer, give the lint filter one last check.
