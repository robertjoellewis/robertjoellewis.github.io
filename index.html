The Mendel Metaphor

🌱 Gregor Mendel (1860s)

Quietly breeding pea plants in a monastery, discovering the laws of inheritance. Rigorous, systematic, ignored for decades.

Then: Three botanists rediscovered his work. Combined with new microscopy. Revolution in genetics.

Result: Norman Borlaug saved over a billion lives using Mendel's "laws"

Entertainment science today = Mendel's pea plants

Academic rigor + new understandings + data analytics + industry needs = Revolution waiting to happen

What Is Entertainment, Really?

❌ Traditional View

Zillmann: "Activity designed to delight and... enlighten through the exhibition of the fortunes or misfortunes of others..."

Vorderer: Cognitive "play"

✅ New Definition

Entertainment as technology for capturing attention

Serves storyteller's goals, not just audience pleasure

Entertainment exists because it benefits the storyteller's goals

The Automobile Metaphor

Cars aren't designed purely for passenger comfort—they serve manufacturer goals

  • Ad-supported cars: Maximize time in vehicle
  • Subscription cars: Maximize fleet turnover
  • Planned obsolescence: Ensure repeat purchases

Similarly:

  • Theatrical films: Pack in thrills, happy endings
  • Streaming series: Cliffhangers, binge-worthiness
  • Branded content: Nudge toward purchase
